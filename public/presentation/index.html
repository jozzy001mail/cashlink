CashLink

Bitcoin Cash payment links for small businesses.
A link, a QR code — and the money goes straight to the merchant.

Non-custodial No account WizardConnect

Owoade · DoraHacks @owoade · github.com/jozzy001mail/cashlink

The problem

Small shops can't take crypto — and cards cost them

  • Card fees, chargebacks and slow settlement eat a market trader's margin.
  • Bitcoin Cash settles in seconds for a fraction of a cent — but there is no dead-simple way for a café or trader to accept it.
  • Existing tools need accounts, custody, or a developer. A shop owner has a phone and a queue of customers.
The solution

CashLink: a payment link, not a platform

1. Merchant creates a link
Amount in USD or BCH, a label, a payout address. Share link + QR code. No account, no custody, no fees from us.

2. Customer pays in BCH
In-app with a WizardConnect wallet (the wallet signs a real BCH mainnet transaction), or with any BCH wallet via a BIP21 QR code.

3. Verified on-chain
CashLink watches the merchant address and marks the invoice paid the moment the transaction lands. Every payment links to a block explorer.

How it works

Real BCH mainnet transactions

  • WizardConnect: QR pairing with Paytaca & other BCH wallets; the dapp derives receive/change addresses from the wallet's xpub — keys never leave the wallet.
  • CashScript: builds the P2PKH transaction (UTXO gathering, placeholder unlocker, change output, 2 sats/byte fee) and produces the WalletConnect transaction object for the wallet to sign and broadcast.
  • Detection: Electrum provider with a Blockchair REST fallback watches the merchant address; paid invoices link to the explorer.
  • Price: live BCH/USD from CoinGecko so merchants can price in USD.
What we proved

Verified — and honestly labelled

  • 11/11 tests pass, including a full local P2PKH build + sign + BCH VM verification with a throwaway key (npm test).
  • Clean production build (npm run build). Invoice logic, BIP21, amount math and fee estimation all unit-tested.
  • Not verifiable from our machine: the live WizardConnect sign step needs a real BCH wallet on a phone — none was available, and the VM blocks outbound WebSockets. The integration follows the official docs exactly; the unsigned transaction it hands the wallet is the same shape the local test proves valid.
Why BCH, why now

Built for the shops, not the speculators

  • BCH fees are sub-cent and settlement is seconds — the only chain where a $5 coffee payment makes economic sense.
  • Nigeria and Africa: huge informal retail, expensive card rails, young phone-first merchants. CashLink meets them where they are.
  • Next: recurring links, multi-item carts, and a merchant dashboard — all on the same non-custodial core.
